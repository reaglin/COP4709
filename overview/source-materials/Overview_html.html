This is what you need to know to get started. 

You will have weekly assignments that are 60% of your grade. I am flexible if you cannot complete these by the due date, and you should attempt to do them, but if you have problems, I will be understanding.

 You have a weekly graded discussion that is 20% of your grade. These must be completed prior to the due date, as they are used for attendance. 

You have 12 quizzes that are  20% of your grade; they have no due date, but they support the material in the assignments. 

If you have problems or questions, send me an email.

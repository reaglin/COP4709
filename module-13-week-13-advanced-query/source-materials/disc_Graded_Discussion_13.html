SQL Puzzles

There are lots of people who create SQL puzzles. SQL puzzles are really good to learn the logic (in SQL) to do complex tasks. A really cool group of puzzles is available here (this is a Github for smpeters who created the puzzles)

https://github.com/smpetersgithub/AdvancedSQLPuzzles/tree/main/Advanced%20SQL%20Puzzles 

You can view the puzzles directly from the pdf on that account here

Link to pdf

Take a look at the first puzzle (and the solution). The puzzle is the best example I have seen to demonstrate how to perform OUTER, LEFT, and RIGHT joins. These puzzles are great learning resources for improving your SQL skill and knowledge.

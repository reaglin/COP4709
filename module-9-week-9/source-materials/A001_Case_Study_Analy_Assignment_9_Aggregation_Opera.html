Assignment 9 - Aggregation Operators

Assignment 9 – SQL Operators (Food Analysis)

Objectives

– To learn to use aggregation functions to analyze data.

– Create a procedure to perform a real database task

Assignment

For this assignment you will use this spreadsheet (supplied by the US Government) Use the following criteria to break the foods into quartiles (4 groups). Once the food is in 4 quartiles, using ONLY foods in the first quartile rank the top foods in correct order of calorie counts.

Healthy – Your final query should return the top 10 healthiest foods based on (1) being in the first quartile of healthy and (2) low calorie count.

Unhealthy – You final query should return the top 10 unhealthiest foods based on being (1) being in the first quartile of unhealthy and  (3) high calorie count

Defining Quartiles

Your stored procedure is going to have to use a specific criteria to define the quartiles. In each case the return set should only consider foods in the top quartile. You are creating a data subset for the remaining query. There will be 2 of these for the requirements of this assignment. 

Quartile Criteria

  1. Fat to Energy (kcal) ratio (Top Quartile)
  2. Fat to Energy (kcal) ratio (Bottom Quartile)

Ranking (Order) Criteria

There are 2 quartiles that you will use as quartile criteria. Within each of these criteria you will rank the top 10 (highest) and bottom 10 (lowest) excluding all 0's ;

  1. Sugar total 
  2. Pick Any Vitamin 

There will be 8 total queries and results;

  1. Fat to Energy top quartile - Sugar top 10
  2. Fat to Energy bottom quartile - Sugar top 10 
  3. Fat to Energy top quartile - Vitamin top 10
  4. Fat to Energy bottom quartile - Vitamin top 10 
  5. Fat to Energy top quartile - Sugar bottom 10
  6. Fat to Energy bottom quartile - Sugar bottom 10 
  7. Fat to Energy top quartile - Vitamin bottom 10
  8. Fat to Energy bottom quartile - Vitamin bottom 10

Grading Criteria
